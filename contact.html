Legal information

Privacy and data policy

When you use our forms we collect only what we need: your name, email address and, for bookings, your phone number, chosen event and any access needs. We use this to reply to you and manage event places. We do not sell your data or use it for marketing without your consent. This prototype does not send form data to a server.

GDPR notice

Under the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, we process your data on the basis of consent and our legitimate interest in running events. You have the right to access, correct or erase your data, to restrict or object to processing and to withdraw consent at any time by emailing hello@superstationery.example. You may complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk). We keep booking data for up to 12 months.

Terms and Conditions

  1. Event places are confirmed by email and are limited.
  2. Please give at least 48 hours’ notice to cancel a booking.
  3. Prices shown include VAT and may change.
  4. Product images are illustrations and may differ from the item.

Cookie notice

This site uses only essential browser storage to remember your cookie choice and your latest booking on this device. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Embedded content from YouTube and Google Maps may set its own cookies when loaded.
