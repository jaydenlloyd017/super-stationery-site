About Super Stationery

Our history

Super Stationery is an independent stationery retailer based in Manchester. We were established ten years ago to offer customers high-quality stationery at affordable prices.

We began as a small local shop selling everyday essentials such as pens, pencils, notebooks, folders and office supplies. As our reputation grew, we expanded into premium stationery, art and craft materials, school supplies, organisational products and business stationery.

Over the last decade we have built a loyal customer base of students, individuals, schools and local businesses.

Our mission

“At Super Stationery, our mission is to provide high-quality, reliable and affordable stationery that helps our customers organise, create and achieve. We aim to combine excellent products with friendly customer service, serving individuals, students, schools and businesses across Manchester and beyond.”
